FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about buying, leasing, and working with our brokerage.

What services does Speed Real Asset provide?

Speed Real Asset is a certified real estate brokerage in Thailand. Under the direction of Mr. Todsapon, we specialize in the purchase, sale, and leasing of condos, detached houses, commercial offices, and land plots with full title verification and loan assistance.

What are the agency commission fees?

For sales transactions, standard commission is 3% of the agreed selling price, payable only upon successful ownership transfer at the Land Office. For rentals, commission is 1 month's rent for a 1-year lease agreement. No hidden upfront fees.

Is title deed inspection free?

Yes, the service is completely free. We thoroughly examine title deeds (Chanote), historical records, and check for existing bank mortgages or encumbrances to ensure a safe, clean transfer.

Can you assist with Thai bank mortgage applications?

Yes. We work directly with mortgage teams across 10+ leading Thai banks (including Bangkok Bank, Kasikornbank, SCB, Krungsri, and others) to help you secure competitive interest rates and suitable loan options.

How can I arrange a property viewing?

We are open daily from 09:00 to 20:00. Property viewings are free of charge. You can schedule by calling 083-879-9635 or connecting on LINE ID: 083-879-9635.

Can foreign nationals buy property in Thailand?

Foreign nationals can purchase condominium units freehold under the Foreign Ownership Quota (up to 49% of the building). For landed properties, foreigners can acquire long-term registered 30-year leases. Our team assists with documentation, fund remittance (FET forms), and Land Department procedures.
